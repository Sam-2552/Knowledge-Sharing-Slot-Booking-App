About the Portal

The Knowledge Sharing Portal helps engineering teams run weekly knowledge sharing sessions. Engineers book slots, share materials, and earn points that feed the quarterly leaderboard.

The portal has been an internal tool since late 2025 and now supports around 300 sessions per quarter across our European offices.
